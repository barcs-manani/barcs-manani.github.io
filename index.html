about

An engineer who builds software, and keeps the rigor.

I'm a full-stack developer currently doing quality assurance for the upcoming e-commerce platform BuyersBounties, validating user flows and documenting application behaviour and bugs. I'm also a Mayerfeld Consultants certified frontend developer (remote). Before that I was part of the team behind Tripzoori, an online travel booking platform, building production software with React and Python FastAPI, designing PostgreSQL models, shipping APIs and helping run the platform in production.

My foundation is a B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering, and it remains part of how I think. Engineering taught me to reason about complex systems, respect real-world constraints and design with reliability in mind. I treat applications as interconnected systems with performance limits and failure modes, not collections of features.

I keep learning on both sides of the stack: large-scale software architecture, cloud deployment and security on one side, and a structured refresh of electronics and embedded fundamentals on the other.
