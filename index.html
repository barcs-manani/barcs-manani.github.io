about

An engineer who moved into software — and kept the rigor.

I'm a full-stack developer currently building the tech behind Tripzoori, an OTA booking platform — Python/FastAPI services, PostgreSQL data models, and React interfaces, deployed and maintained in production.

My background is a B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering, not Computer Science — and I've found that's an asset rather than a detour. Years spent reasoning about circuits, signal integrity, and embedded constraints translate directly into how I think about software: as systems with real limits, failure modes, and performance trade-offs, not just features to ship.

Outside of core development work, I'm continually adding depth — currently studying large-scale software architecture and working toward CompTIA Security+, alongside earlier, hands-on data analytics and automation work.
